# konstats: Open Course in Statistics for Linguists

Materials of two open courses in statistics for linguists, Statistics
and Advanced Statistics, which are also taught at the Department of
Linguistics, University of Konstanz. Statistics follows Winter (2019)
from data wrangling to mixed models. Advanced Statistics covers
generalised additive mixed models, random forests, power analysis,
factor analysis and cluster analysis. The data sets of the courses are
in the simling package.

## Details

Before the first session,
[`install_course()`](https://mcanzi.de/konstats/reference/install_course.md)
installs the packages of a course and
[`check_setup()`](https://mcanzi.de/konstats/reference/check_setup.md)
tells you whether anything is missing.
[`course_packages()`](https://mcanzi.de/konstats/reference/course_packages.md)
lists the packages and what each is used for.

[`lectures()`](https://mcanzi.de/konstats/reference/lectures.md) and
[`slides()`](https://mcanzi.de/konstats/reference/slides.md) find the
slides of a lecture, and
[`get_tutorial()`](https://mcanzi.de/konstats/reference/get_tutorial.md)
copies the exercise sheet of a tutorial into your project. The weekly
assignment starts from the *Weekly assignment* template (*File \> New
File \> R Markdown \> From Template* in RStudio).

[`resid_acf()`](https://mcanzi.de/konstats/reference/resid_acf.md),
[`partial_dependence()`](https://mcanzi.de/konstats/reference/partial_dependence.md),
[`cv_speakers()`](https://mcanzi.de/konstats/reference/cv_speakers.md)
and
[`theme_paper()`](https://mcanzi.de/konstats/reference/theme_paper.md)
are the helpers written in the Advanced Statistics slides.
